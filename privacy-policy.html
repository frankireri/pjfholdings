Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026 | PJF HOLDINGS LTD (Shimba Hills, Kwale County, Kenya)

1. Overview

PJF HOLDINGS LTD ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our customers, website visitors, and farm partners in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Information We Collect

When you request quotes, advisory consultations, or equipment inquiries through pjfholdings.co.ke or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: Full name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address.
  • Farm Information: Farm location, acreage size, primary crops, water source, and irrigation/solar requirements.
  • Technical Log Data: IP address, browser type, and anonymous usage patterns to optimize website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively to:

  • Provide tailored engineering quotes for solar water pumps and drip irrigation layouts.
  • Schedule agronomy site surveys and farm consultations across Kwale County and Coastal Kenya.
  • Provide technical support and scheduled maintenance for farm machinery.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or farm data to third parties. Information is securely stored and accessed only by authorized PJF Holdings engineering and advisory staff.
